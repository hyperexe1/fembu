# Fembu — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fembu collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through its verification system.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the verification process, Fembu may collect:

- Discord username, user ID, or related account information.
- Information submitted through the verification form.
- Verification status and verification-related records.
- Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

Fembu will only request information that is reasonably necessary for the verification process.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Process verification requests.
- Confirm eligibility for verification.
- Prevent fraud, impersonation, and abuse.
- Maintain verification records.
- Protect the security of Fembu.
- Respond to support requests.

## 3. Verification Material

If Fembu requires additional verification material, such as an image or other evidence, it will only be used for the stated verification purpose.

Fembu should collect and retain the minimum information necessary for verification.

## 4. Data Retention

Verification information will not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required for legitimate security, legal, or operational reasons.

Where possible, unnecessary verification material will be deleted after the verification process is completed.

## 5. Data Sharing

Fembu does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the verification service.
- Prevent abuse or fraud.
- Protect the security of Fembu.
- Comply with applicable legal requirements.

Fembu does not intentionally make verification information publicly available.

## 6. Data Security

Fembu takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Fembu may use third-party services to operate parts of its infrastructure. Information handled by those services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 8. User Requests

Where applicable, users may request information about personal data held by Fembu or request correction or deletion of information.

Requests can be made through the official Fembu contact method.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Fembu is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted improperly, contact the Fembu administration team.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## 11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Fembu administration team through the official Fembu contact method.

By using Fembu's verification system, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.
